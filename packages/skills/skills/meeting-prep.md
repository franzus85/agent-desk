---
name: meeting-prep
description: Prepare briefing notes for an upcoming meeting using the calendar, notes, and the wiki.
requires: [calendar.search, notes.search, wiki.search, notes.write]
---

## Steps
1. Find the meeting in the calendar by searching its title.
2. Search notes for anything related to the meeting's topic or attendees.
3. Search the wiki for related background (architecture, roadmap, onboarding context).
4. Write a short prep note summarizing what was found.

## Verify before finishing
- The prep note references the meeting's actual date and attendees, not invented ones.
- Every claim in the prep note traces back to a note or wiki page found in steps 2-3.
